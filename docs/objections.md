# Objections

The five objections this model always meets, in the reader's voice, with the evidence one click away.

## "Epstein-Zin preferences are exotic."

They are the minimal structure that separates risk aversion from the elasticity of intertemporal substitution. Power utility welds the two together, and log utility prices the value premium at exactly zero. This site is an existence proof, not a truth claim — see the scoping line on [Home]({{ '/' | relative_url }}).

## "Consumption-based pricing failed."

The failure was power utility pricing short-run betas (Hansen and Jagannathan 1991). Here cash flows load on the persistent component of consumption, and returns never enter estimation. The evidence is [Measuring leverage]({{ '/measuring-leverage.html' | relative_url }}).

## "Firms aren't consumption claims."

Any dividend process can be projected onto the consumption moving average; that is the entire estimation step. Bring your own: [Price your own claim]({{ '/price-your-own-claim.html' | relative_url }}).

## "We already calibrate a discount rate."

A calibrated level is not a cross-section. This model supplies the dispersion across claims; a single rate cannot. The demonstration is [Two free numbers]({{ '/two-free-numbers.html' | relative_url }}).

## "This is too academic to implement."

Every number on this site regenerates from committed scripts. Installation to first valuation: ten seconds, measured. Start at [Installation]({{ '/installation.html' | relative_url }}).
