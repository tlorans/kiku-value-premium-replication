# Is the Value Premium a Puzzle?

High book-to-market firms earned about 6 percent more per year than low book-to-market firms over 1930–2003. Market betas of the two portfolios are essentially the same, so the CAPM does not price the spread (Kiku 2006).

Kiku places value, growth, and the market in the long-run risks model of Bansal and Yaron (2004) with Epstein–Zin preferences. Value dividends load more on the persistent component of consumption growth than growth dividends do. With $$\rho=0.98$$ that gap produces a model value premium of about 5.3 percent, a lower price–dividend ratio for value, and CAPM and C-CAPM betas that still fail.
